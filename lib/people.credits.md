# Credits — the invented people (site/people/<product>/ on the CDN, lib/people.json)

**AI-generated with Orazaka (FLUX.2 [klein], Apache-2.0), fictional people, owned by Krizaka.**

Every portrait was **generated by Orazaka** on one Mac (MacBook Pro M1 Max, 64 GB), locally — nothing left the machine.

- **Model**: FLUX.2 [klein] 4B (Black Forest Labs), Q8 GGUF — **Apache-2.0** — https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B
- **Text encoder**: Qwen3-4B (Alibaba Qwen) — Apache-2.0. **VAE**: FLUX.2 VAE — Apache-2.0.
- **Runtime**: stable-diffusion.cpp (MIT) on Metal, through Orazaka's `image-generation` Studio (`orazaka studio run image-generation --studio`), 1024×1024, 4 steps; published as WebP 512 and 256.
- **Account**: Eric, the demo persona.
- **People**: fictional adults. No real person, celebrity look-alike, brand or logo is depicted; every image was reviewed and none needed regenerating.
- **Rule**: one face = one persona = one product (`tests/people.test.mjs`). The faces of the Krizaka UI examples are used by no other subject.
