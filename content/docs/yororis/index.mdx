> **Under construction.** We're building Yororis in the open and studying its framework — legal, ethical, clinical — before making it available. Nothing is live, nothing is offered, and there is no date.

## What Yororis is

**A listening space with a face and a voice.** A person opens a *moment* with a *presence*: an AI presence with a first
name, an animated portrait that carries a permanent "AI" mark, a voice and exact subtitles. The presence listens,
reflects back, asks one question at a time and helps put words on what is being lived.

- **A presence that stops.** A moment lasts up to 25 minutes; there is one a day and three a week by default. The
  organisation that offers Yororis sets these limits for its members, the server applies them, the screen shows them.
- **What I keep.** When the moment closes, the transcript is gone. What remains is three lines the person reads, edits
  or deletes, and the mood they gave themselves before and after — that becomes **the thread** of the next moment.
- **One page to bring along.** The person can export what they kept as one page, for the people who support them.

## What Yororis is not

- Not care: Yororis makes no health claim and replaces no one who supports you.
- Not an emergency service: in a crisis, people trained to listen answer right away.
- Not a person: an AI presence that says so, every time.

Not care, not an emergency service, not a person. In a crisis in Canada, call or text 9-8-8 — in Québec, 811 (Info-Social).

## The controls it inherits from Orazaka

Every AI call goes through **Orazaka's wellbeing pack** — Yororis has no other AI provider, and the guards live in the
engine, not in a prompt ([ADR-055](https://github.com/krizaka/orazaka/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-055-regulated-controls-and-the-wellbeing-pack.md)).

| Control | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| Versioned consent | No consent to the current statement, no moment: the engine answers `451` with the statement itself. |
| Crisis guard | Runs first, cannot be switched off; the answer is fixed and reviewed, with a sourced and dated regional line — never generated. |
| Scope guard | What is outside a listening space is declined, word for word. |
| Age attestation | 18+, recorded with the consent. |
| Retention and audit | 30 days (shorter on request), encryption, an append-only audit. |

Yororis adds its own: the limits applied by the server, the transcript destroyed at the summary, a mood that is only
ever self-reported (nothing is inferred), export and deletion by the person, aggregate statistics only.

## What is being studied

Questions, not conclusions — answered with a lawyer before anything is offered to anyone:

- **Québec** — the reserved title and act; Law 25: privacy impact, consent, retention, hosting.
- **Canada** — Health Canada's rules for software; federal privacy law; what may be said to organisations.
- **France and the European Union** — the AI Act (telling people they talk to an AI, reading emotions); GDPR article 9.
- **Belgium** — the same European questions, and the crisis lines of both language communities.
- **Everywhere** — the conditions of a pilot with outside people, and insurance.
